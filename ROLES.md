# Specialist roles

Four friendly roles are available in Army recruitment. Five enemy specialists replace ordinary attackers in later waves; total authored wave sizes are unchanged.

| Friendly role | Cost | Behavior |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Scout | 6 | Fast, lightly armed observer. Spots concealed enemies within 420 pixels when terrain, weather, light and smoke permit. Observed mortar targets receive half the normal scatter. Can patrol. |
| Heavy Machine Gun Crew | 12 | Tripod M1919 with sustained suppression. Packs for 2 seconds before moving, deploys for 3 seconds and covers a 70-degree arc. Automatically traverses and redeploys for targets outside that arc. |
| Assault Infantry | 8 | Thompson SMG and six resuppliable grenades. Issue a building movement/garrison order against hostile occupants to approach an entrance, conduct close combat and occupy the requested position once cleared. Movement orders cancel clearing. |
| Anti-Tank Gun Crew | 18 | Long-range 57 mm gun prioritizing vehicles. Packs for 3 seconds, deploys for 4 seconds and covers a 50-degree arc. Slower traverse than a machine gun. Cannot run or garrison. |

Weapon crews deploy automatically when stationary and unpinned. Selected crews show their firing arcs, packing and deployment status. They cannot run or use Run to Gunfire. Smoke and flanking remain effective counters to machine guns.

| Enemy role | First wave | Behavior and counter |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Machine Gun Team | 3 | Halts to deploy an MG 42 and suppress the line. Use smoke, mortars or flank its firing arc. |
| Field Officer | 5 | Boosts nearby enemy morale recovery and helps them recover from pinning. Eliminate the officer to remove that support. |
| Anti-Tank Infantry | 6 | Hunts friendly vehicles and spends 2 seconds aiming before launching a Panzerschreck rocket. Movement, pinning or a new target interrupts aiming. Screen trucks with infantry. |
| Sharpshooter | 8 | Prefers support troops, holds at long range and is harder to detect until firing. Shots expose it for 4 seconds. Scouts, flares and close patrols reveal it. |
| Infiltrator | 9 | Chooses a flank and favors foliage while avoiding defenders and flare light. Bypasses distant troops without firing; fights when intercepted nearby. Use scouts and patrols to detect gaps being exploited. |

New roles retain existing morale, terrain, personal weapon upgrades, experience and casualty systems. Their deployment, detection, aiming and building-clearing state is included in validated saves. All five enemy specialists remain in the final wave and endless-wave composition.

## Field abilities

Select one specialist in the Squad panel to use its field command. Movement cancels an established duty. Duties, cooldowns, remaining stock, and scout reports survive saving and reloading; positional duties are cleared when soldiers leave the sector.

| Role | Command | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Officer | Rally within 160px for 12 seconds; 45-second cooldown | Must remain stationary and unpinned. Improves morale and suppression recovery without guaranteeing recovery under overwhelming fire. |
| Scout | Observation post: 540px, or 620px from a building position, before visibility modifiers | Holds fire. Last observed locations fade over 12 seconds and never provide live targeting information. |
| Supply officer | Forward supply: 3 points buy 12 individual grenades, rockets, or mortar bombs | Supplies one item every 2 seconds within 90px. Cannot run delivery errands while posted; moving abandons the remaining stock. |
| Engineer | Nearby repair queue: damaged standing buildings within 160px, most damaged first | 1 point buys 40 health over 4 seconds. Pauses while engaged, suppressed, or constructing. Destroyed buildings cannot be restored. |
| MG / heavy MG | Suppress a marked 55px area for 10 seconds; 20-second cooldown | Suppression-only fire requires visibility, range, and a clear firing arc. Heavy crews must deploy. Smoke, pinning, and movement interrupt fire. |

Wet mud and water impose extra movement and reload penalties; vehicles suffer more than infantry. Ruined building footprints provide ordinary small-arms cover to infantry and slow infantry by 20% and vehicles by 35%. Hover buildings for landmark-specific advice; campaign briefings explain the ground and support options.
